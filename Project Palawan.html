Palawan: The Last Frontier

Palawan is a long, narrow archipelagic province known as the “Last Frontier” of the Philippines. It is famous for its pristine beaches, dramatic limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, underground rivers, rich biodiversity, and crystal-clear waters.

Located in the westernmost part of the Philippines, Palawan stretches between the South China Sea and the Sulu Sea, making it one of the country's most unique island destinations.

Why We Selected Palawan

Palawan was chosen because it is widely regarded as one of the most beautiful and unique tourist destinations in the Philippines and the world. It offers a perfect combination of natural wonders, adventure activities, and cultural experiences that make it ideal for promotion as a top eco-tourism destination.

  • Unspoiled beaches and hidden coves
  • World-class island landscapes and limestone formations
  • Rich biodiversity and marine life
  • Excellent eco-tourism and adventure opportunities

Why El Nido?

El Nido is one of Palawan’s most iconic destinations, celebrated for its dramatic limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, and breathtaking island-hopping experiences. It captures the true beauty of Palawan in one unforgettable setting, making it a standout symbol of the province’s natural charm.

The destination is a perfect representation of what makes Palawan special: stunning scenery, adventure, and a connection to nature that few places can match.
